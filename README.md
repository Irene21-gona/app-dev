## 🌟 Overview
**Stranger Things** is an American science fiction horror drama series. Set in the 1980s in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana, the narrative centers around the disappearance of a young boy and the appearance of a girl with psychokinetic abilities.

---

## 👥 Key Characters

Here are some of the main characters in the series:

1. **Eleven** – A mysterious girl with telekinetic powers *(Protagonist)*
2. **Mike Wheeler** – Intelligent and dedicated group leader
3. **Dustin Henderson** – The quick-witted science enthusiast
4. **Lucas Sinclair** – Pragmatic and loyal defender of the group
5. **Will Byers** – The boy whose disappearance triggers the main plot
6. **Jim Hopper** – Hawkins' chief of police
